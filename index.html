Understanding Clustering Inputs

Not every column in the dataset should influence how HCPs are grouped.

📈

Clustering Inputs

Behavioural and business variables such as prescription volume, product-level prescribing, engagement, specialty, region and therapy area. Only selected features are used.

🪪

Reference Fields

HCP IDs, names and other identification fields are retained for reporting and record mapping. They are not used in distance calculations.

⚠️

Existing Business Labels

Existing tiers, categories or segment labels are excluded by default to avoid biasing the newly generated segments.
